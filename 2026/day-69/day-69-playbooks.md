Day 69 – Ansible Playbooks and Modules
Overview

Day 69 focused on building reusable and idempotent automation with Ansible playbooks.

Learned how Ansible modules, handlers, and tasks work together.

Concepts Used
Concept	Used For
Playbooks	Automation workflows
Tasks	Units of work
Modules	System management operations
Handlers	Triggered service restarts
Idempotency	Safe repeatable automation
Multi-Play Playbooks	Group-specific automation
Key Learnings
Installed and configured Nginx through playbooks
Used handlers for controlled service restarts
Practiced modules like yum, copy, file, and service
Used register and debug
Practiced dry-run and diff mode
Built multi-server playbooks
